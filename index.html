About MATMEG Steel Brokers

MATMEG Steel Brokers is a steel brokerage connecting businesses with reliable steel products and processing services. We specialise in sourcing, cutting, bending and supplying steel products to specification. Our network of suppliers and processors allows us to deliver practical solutions for construction, manufacturing and industrial projects.

Whether you require specialised components or bulk materials, MATMEG Steel Brokers, with its strong industry relationships and market knowledge, is able to connect you with the right steel solutions.
